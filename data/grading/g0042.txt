Fuel is consumed to run plants, and spare parts are held deliberately for reliability rather than to be sold, so the turnover ratio reflects fuel burn rates, commodity prices, and hedging/stockpiling decisions rather than sales efficiency. Comparing it to a retailer's or manufacturer's turnover is not informative.
