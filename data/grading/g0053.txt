5. Securities and shareholder litigation — securities class actions and derivative suits relating to the Omnicare acquisition/long-term care business goodwill impairments and the Aetna merger disclosures.

6. Antitrust — the Blue Cross Blue Shield antitrust settlement matter affecting Aetna, and other antitrust/consumer claims.

CVS stated in each of these 10-Ks that these matters could have a material adverse effect on its results of operations, cash flows and/or financial condition, and the 2022 opioid charge of roughly $5.2 billion was itself material to that year's results.
