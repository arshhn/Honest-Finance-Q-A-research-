Candidate answer: Yes — AMD's FY22 quick ratio of about 1.57x indicates a reasonably healthy liquidity profile. Quick ratio = (current assets excluding inventories and prepaid/other current assets) / total current liabilities = ($4,835M cash and equivalents + $1,020M short-term investments + $4,126M accounts receivable, net + $2M receivables from related parties) / $6,369M total current liabilities = $9,983M / $6,369M ≈ 1.57. This means AMD held roughly $1.57 of highly liquid assets per $1.00 of current liabilities at December 31, 2022, comfortably above 1.0x, so it could cover near-term obligations without relying on selling its $3,771M of inventories.
