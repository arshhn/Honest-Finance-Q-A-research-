General and administrative expenses increased due to company expansion and increased personnel. Interest income rose significantly in 1997, largely due to higher average cash balances from the company's initial public offering and a private placement to Baxter.

Government grant revenue was essentially flat, approximately $660,000 in 1997 versus $758,000 in 1996. General and administrative expenses were approximately $3.2 million in 1997 versus $2.2 million in 1996 (up about $1.0 million). Interest income increased to approximately $1.2 million in 1997 from $482,000 in 1996 on higher average cash balances following the IPO and the Baxter private placement, while interest expense was roughly unchanged at about $15,000 in 1997 versus $18,000 in 1996.
